# SEO evidence contract

## Source hierarchy

1. Direct first-party search-performance data is primary for current organic clicks, impressions, CTR, position, and indexed reality.
2. A governed SEO provider is supporting evidence for expansion, SERPs, competitors, backlinks, ranks, and crawl output.
3. Rendered production checks establish current technical substance.
4. An authorized browser visibility probe is additive and does not establish search performance or LLM citations by inference.

## Query classification

For query-level quick-win or page-refresh analysis, a local deterministic
classifier may label rows before query-level CTR or ranking calculations. It
is evidence hygiene, not a new source or attribution system.

- Exclude known `artefact`, `pivot`, `tracker_probe`, `agent_harness`, and `pasted_string` rows from query-level opportunity scoring.
- Keep `conversational_candidate` rows in a separate cohort. They can nominate an answer-block review only when the canonical owner, intent, value rationale, and normal search-performance evidence also support it.
- Never call a label proof that a query came from an AI surface, a person, or a bot.
- Record classifier name and version, rule coverage, excluded-row count, candidate count, and whether a relevant generative-search export was available.

Third-party or model-based classification is opt-in only. It must name its
provider, data handling, confidence threshold, and coverage. Its output stays
additive and cannot override first-party evidence, canonical ownership, or
content approval requirements.

Record source, capture date, freshness, comparable window, scope, row or
coverage limits, and availability in every decision. Do not report a stale
crawl as live technical health, an unavailable source as zero, or a partial
result as complete.
