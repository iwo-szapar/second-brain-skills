# Canonical-owner contract

One query family has one active canonical owner. Supporting pages may exist
only when they have a different reader job, primary keyword, opening answer,
and internal-link role.

Before recommending `create`, record:

1. Current matching URLs and their intended jobs.
2. Open work items and active content plans.
3. The selected owner URL and why it is not already sufficient.
4. Supporting URLs, if any, and their bounded roles.
5. Link, redirect, canonical, and measurement implications.

If this record cannot be made, default to `refresh`, `expand`, `defend`, or
`blocked-by-evidence`; do not create a speculative duplicate.
