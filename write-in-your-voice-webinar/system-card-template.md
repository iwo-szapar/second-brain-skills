# System Card template

Complete this after reviewing the Draft Contract. Keep the fields specific
enough that a future draft can pass or fail them.

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Job + trigger | What repeats, and when should the system run? |
| 2. Reader + decision | Who is the reader, and what should they decide or do next? |
| 3. Approved sources | Which material may inform a draft? What stays out? |
| 4. Standard 1 | Source evidence, pass test, and failure test. |
| 5. Standard 2 | Source evidence, pass test, and failure test. |
| 6. Standards 3–5 | Add only standards supported by the sources. |
| 7. Workflow + output | Ordered steps, exact artifact, facts, and gaps. |
| 8. Checks + boundaries | What counts as usable? What must not happen? |
| 9. Known unknowns | Missing facts or preferences that remain visible. |
| 10. Correction + proof | One approved correction and the next comparable brief. |

For every proposed standard, the owner chooses **keep**, **change**, or
**remove**. Record a decision for Standards 1–5, including each substandard in
Field 6. A Draft Contract never becomes permanent instructions by itself.
