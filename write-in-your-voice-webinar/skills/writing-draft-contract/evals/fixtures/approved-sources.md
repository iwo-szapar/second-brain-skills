# Approved examples for a weekly founder update

## Example one

This week we decided to pause the partner rollout. We had two relevant customer
interviews, not enough evidence for the original promise, and a clearer
question: does the onboarding brief reduce handover time? Next week we will
test that question with three active users.

## Example two

I do not send a list of activities. I name one decision, what evidence changed
it, and what I will test next. If a number is not in the source notes, I leave
it out rather than make the update sound more certain.

## Example three

The reader is a collaborator who needs to understand the decision and its next
check. The update is short, plain, and specific. It does not claim a result
before a test has happened.
